Who we are

We buy the gold you no longer wear.

Families come to us with old sets, broken pieces, inherited coins and jewellery they do not want to keep. Our job is simple: test it in the room, explain the number, and pay the same day if you accept.

Real Gold Buyer is a buying desk. We do not sell new jewellery. We do not mix design value into the offer. You are paid for purity and weight against the live rate — nothing else is added, nothing is quietly taken off.

That is why people return: the first figure on the slip is the figure that is paid. You can photograph every reading. If the offer does not suit you, you leave with your gold.
